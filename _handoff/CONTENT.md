## SECTION 3 — Menu Highlights

**Eyebrow:** WHAT'S GOOD

**Heading (H2):** A menu built around what people actually order.

**Intro line:** Categories below are a taste. Full menu and daily specials at the table.

### Card grid (6 cards):

**1. Wings**
Made the way you like them — plain, tossed, sauced, dry-rubbed. Mulligan's Secret Recipe, garlic parmesan, mild, hot, and the rest of the lineup. Flats-only or drums-only available.

**2. Burgers & Sandwiches**
Award-winning burgers off the grill. Reubens, Cubans, French dips, Philly cheesesteaks, buffalo chicken wraps. Served with house-made chips; swap to fries, tots, sweet potato fries, or onion rings.

**3. Hand-Cut Steaks**
Sirloin, ribeye, and the occasional NY strip special — cut in-house and grilled to order. Ask about tonight's steak special.

**4. Seafood**
Hand-dipped fish & chips, fried shrimp, and a Mahi Reuben that has its own fan club. Daily fresh catch when available.

**5. Apps & Shareables**
Loaded tots and loaded fries, jalapeño poppers, fried mushrooms, hot wings, lobster roll, beer cheese soup, French onion soup. Or do a Pick-3 combo and try a little of everything.

**6. Salads & Wraps**
Garden, chef, taco salad in a fried tortilla shell, buffalo chicken wrap, steak quesadilla. Grilled, blackened, or fried chicken on any salad.
