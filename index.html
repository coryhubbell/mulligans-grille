Our Story

How did this Mulligan thing start?

You might think it started in Ireland — it’s an Irish name, after all. Nope. The mulligan started on a golf course, the do-over you take when the first shot didn’t go your way.

One story: a Canadian golfer named David Mulligan at the Country Club of Montreal in the 1920s — jumpy on the first tee, hit a poor shot, re-teed, called the second one a “correction shot.” The other story: John “Buddy” Mulligan, a locker-room attendant at Essex Fells Country Club in New Jersey in the 1930s, who’d play after his shift and figured he’d earned an extra shot. The members started doing it too, and the name stuck.

That’s the spirit of this place. Pull up a chair, take your second shot at a good night, and order the burger.

About

A neighborhood grill, the way it should be.

New owners: Tracy & Bobby Parks

Mulligan’s has been a Port Orange fixture for years — the kind of place where the regulars have a seat and the first-timers leave like regulars. We keep it simple: amazing wings made the way you like them, award-winning burgers, hand-cut steaks, and the Hole-In-One Burger that has its own fan club.

Our sauces, dressings, and signature dishes are made from scratch in the kitchen. Pull up a stool at the bar, grab a booth with the family, or settle into the Courtyard and Bar — there’s room for whatever kind of night you’re having.
